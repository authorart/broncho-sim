# Glossary

**Segment** — A segmental bronchus (Boyden B1–B10 per side; on the left B1+2 and B7+8 share trunks). The unit trainees navigate to and identify.

**Subsegment** — A branch beyond a segment. In BronchoSim, subsegments are shown but never used as targets.

**Station** — An IASLC lymph node station (for example 4R or 7). Not to be confused with a Landmark.

**Landmark** — A named intraluminal view used for teaching (for example the main carina or the RUL trifurcation).

**Wedge** — The scope tip filling a 3rd–4th order bronchus so that the distal airway is isolated. It is required for BAL and is a protective manoeuvre after TBLB. **Over-wedging** means the tip is forced and traumatises the mucosa.

**Wall contact** — The scope tip striking the airway wall during advancement (a mucosal-trauma proxy). It counts against a Task score.

**Task** — A scored simulator exercise of one of two kinds: *navigate* (drive to a target Segment) or *identify* (name the Segment in the centre of a frozen view).

**Attempt** — One completed Task or quiz answer as recorded in a learner's progress.

**Mini-simulation** — The scored interactive exercise attached to a Procedure. It is distinct from the Simulator, which only drives the scope.

**Procedure** — One of six sampling techniques: BW, BAL, brush, EBBx, TBLB or TBNA/EBUS-TBNA.

**Non-diagnostic** — A sampling result that does not establish a diagnosis. It is preferred over "negative" because TBNA lacks a 100% negative predictive value.

**Standard view** — A named endoscopic view whose orientation matches the reference atlas photograph (Olympus nomenclature poster). Right-sided views are shown rotated, as they appear after the usual scope rotation.

**Secondary carina** — A spur between lobar or segmental orifices: RC1 separates the RUL and the bronchus intermedius; RC2 separates the RML and RLL; LC1 separates the upper division and the lingula; LC2 separates the LUL and LLL.

**Common trunk** — A short bronchus shared by two segments before they divide, for example B9+10 (both sides) or the left B1+2 and B7+8.
